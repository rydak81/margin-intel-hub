# Visual engagement and return visits

## Research basis

Reviewed MarketplaceBeta production and The Verge / Linear visually, and Retail Dive, Axios, Feedly and TechCrunch through their public pages. These are inspiration sources, not designs or content to copy.

- The Verge: strong lead-story hierarchy, topic collections and a separate latest/following stream. Apply varied emphasis and clear routes into recurring topics. https://www.theverge.com/
- Retail Dive: operational topic navigation, deep dives, library, events and specialized newsletters. Apply a visible route from a headline to a relevant operating task. https://www.retaildive.com/
- Linear: restrained visual depth, consistent surfaces, product previews and clear workspace entry. Apply a functional dashboard preview and predictable controls. https://linear.app/
- Feedly: organize intelligence around a repeatable monitoring workflow. Saved views are a first small step; backend alerts and team workspaces are not delivered in this pass. https://feedly.com/

Semrush estimates for August 2026 (accessed October 9): The Verge 8.96 million visits; TechCrunch 10.3 million visits. These are estimated visits, not unique readers, audited publisher figures or a prediction for MarketplaceBeta. They do not establish that a particular design or feature caused the traffic.

## Implemented scope

- Widen major news, dashboard, tools, guide, events and fee-hub layouts to a 1600px cap. Keep reading text and narrow forms bounded. Wide screens carry more useful layout; mobile stays single-column where appropriate.
- Make Dashboard explicit in shared navigation and add a persistent dashboard entry, including mobile.
- Homepage: real-data briefing coverage panel linking to platform-filtered dashboard views; primary dashboard CTA; actual news before evergreen guides. Counts describe only the homepage briefing and are not popularity or marketplace share.
- Shared decorative geometry, stronger dark/light contrast, raised panels, restrained hover motion and reduced-motion support. New exploration cards provide news -> research -> tools -> guides paths on shared public pages.
- Save one dashboard filter view locally on the reader's browser, restore it on a later unfiltered visit, forget it explicitly, and bookmark/share filtered URLs. Explicit URL filters take precedence. Stored values are bounded and validated; denied/malformed storage does not disable filtering. This is not account synchronization or an alert service.
