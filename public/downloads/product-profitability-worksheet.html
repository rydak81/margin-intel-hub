Use a consistent fee base. Expected returns loss = return probability × average unrecovered loss, excluding costs already counted elsewhere. Example: 5% × $20 = $1 per order.

Contribution = selling price − total variable costs.
Contribution margin = contribution ÷ selling price × 100.
Do not label contribution as net business profit.
