## Signal: the character

Signal is a visibly nonhuman research companion: a small graphite instrument with a single cyan optical lens and a chartreuse indicator. Curious, concise and methodical. Its recurring visual action is illuminating an evidence card. It does not wear a lab coat, claim a doctorate, impersonate a human analyst or invent personal experiences.

Use Ryan’s actual photo on his personal profile. Use Signal as a series marker, explainer character, report watermark or brand-account avatar. The mascot makes the work recognizable; the evidence makes it credible.

Visual system: midnight navy #08131B, cyan #69E5F0, chartreuse #D9F52B, warm white #F3F4ED. One strong headline, one useful chart or artifact, one visible source/date line. Exact charts are rendered from verified data, never generated as decorative fake numbers. Keep mobile text large and the design consistent.

## Voice

Plainspoken, curious, commercially practical, occasionally dry. Ryan can sound like a Southern business owner without becoming a caricature. A useful signature line is: **“Interesting. Now show me what changes for the business.”** Use it sparingly.

Write an original observation before summarizing sources. Use specific nouns, explicit units and one clear consequence. Be willing to say a result is inconclusive. Avoid superlatives, invented authority, generic inspiration, fake urgency and unsupported revenue claims. No fake personal testing, engagement pods, automated comments or manufactured controversy.
